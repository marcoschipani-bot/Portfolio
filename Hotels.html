Property Highlight (The Americas)

The Fogo Island Inn, Canada

  • Price (Per Night): Starting at $1,296.45
  • Rating: Relais & Châteaux
  • Amenities: Food and excursions are included in the nightly room rate. Guests receive a complimentary car to use during their stay or can opt to have a chauffeur.

Property Highlight (Europe)

The Rosewood, London

  • Price (Per Night): Starting at $697.00
  • Rating: 5 Star Hotel
  • Amenities: Guests can choose between 6 restaurants and may receive spa treatments at an additional cost. All rooms have access to 24/hr room service in addition to a butler service.

Property Highlight (Asia)

Beniya Mukayu, Kaga-shi, Japan

  • Price (Per Night): Starting at $641.00
  • Rating: Relais & Châteaux
  • Amenities: Guests have access to a Buddhist Temple, 2 hot springs, and 3 libraries. All rooms are stocked with the finest Japanese toiletries and luxury bedding. This property offers a fine-dining restaurant that overlooks the grounds luscious Forest Garden.
