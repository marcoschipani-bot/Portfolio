Getting to The Americas

Hubs In Europe: Flying to destinations across North, Central, or South America can be easily facilitated through one of the many European hubs. Daily non-stop flights are offered by budget and premium carriers from major European cities to major North and South American cities including Sao Paulo and Tamarindo.

Hubs In Asia: Getting to the Americas from Asia will be exhausting but non-stop flights are offered from many major cities. Some frequented flights from Asia to the Americas include Tokyo-New York, Singapore-Los Angeles, and Hong Kong-New-York. Several budget and premium carriers offer flights to South America mostly headed for Brazil specifically, Sao Paulo or Rio de Janeiro.

Pricing Options (London-New York)

  • Budget: London, Heathrow-New-York, John F. Kennedy $450.00 Via Tap Air Portugal (1 stop)
  • Convenience: London, Gatwick-New-York, John F. Kennedy $575.00 Via British Airways (Non-stop)
  • Premium: NetJets, London-New York starting at $50,000 (Any airports)

Getting to Europe

Hubs In The Americas: Flying to destinations across Europe can be easily facilitated through one of the many American hubs. Daily non-stop flights are offered by various carriers from major American cities to major European cities. Non-stop flights to France are accessible through major hubs, including New York's JFK, Chicago O'Hare, or Atlanta.

Hubs In Asia: Getting to Europe from Asia can be demanding, but non-stop flights are available from many major cities. Some popular routes from Asia to Europe include Tokyo-London, Singapore-Paris, and Hong Kong-Frankfurt. Several carriers offer flights to Southern Europe, often headed to Spain or Italy, specifically Madrid or Rome.

Pricing Options (Shanghai-Paris)

  • Convenience: Shanghai, Pudong-Paris, CDG $1,360.00 Via Air France (Nonstop)
  • Premium: NetJets, Shanghai-Paris starting at $125,000 (Any Airports)
